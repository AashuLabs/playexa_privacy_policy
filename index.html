Privacy Policy for PlayExa Video Player

Effective Date: 10 August 2026

PlayExa Video Player ("the App", "we", "our", or "us") is a media player application designed to play videos stored locally on your device. We are committed to protecting your privacy and ensuring a transparent, safe user experience.

This Privacy Policy explains our data practices, including permissions used for local video playback and advertising services. By using PlayExa Video Player, you agree to the terms outlined in this policy.

1. Local Media Privacy & Information Collection

PlayExa Video Player is designed with local media privacy as a top priority. Your local videos, audio tracks, and personal media files are never collected, stored, or transmitted to external servers.

  • Zero Cloud Storage: The App does not upload, back up, or transmit your media files to any remote server or cloud service.
  • No Account Required: The App operates without requiring user accounts or registration.
  • Local Processing: All media discovery, thumbnail generation, and playback rendering occur entirely on your local device.

2. Third-Party Advertising (Google AdMob & UMP)

To support ongoing development while keeping the application free, PlayExa Video Player integrates Google AdMob to serve banner advertisements in the main video list.

  • Google Mobile Ads SDK (AdMob): Google Mobile Ads SDK (AdMob) may automatically collect and process certain information, which may include IP address, device or account identifiers, user interaction information, and diagnostic information, for purposes such as providing and measuring advertising, analytics, fraud prevention, and related services. For details on how Google handles advertising data, visit Google Privacy & Terms.
  • User Messaging Platform (UMP) & Consent Management: We use Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP) SDK to facilitate user consent management in compliance with applicable privacy regulations (such as GDPR in the European Economic Area, UK, and Switzerland). Users can review or manage their ad consent preferences at any time via the "Privacy & Consent Settings" option in the App's Settings menu.

3. Permissions Usage

PlayExa Video Player requests standard Android permissions necessary for local media discovery and online banner ad delivery:

  • READ_MEDIA_VIDEO (Android 13 and above): Exclusively used to discover and display local video files on your device.
  • READ_EXTERNAL_STORAGE (Android 12 and below): Exclusively used to discover and display local video files on your device.
  • INTERNET & ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE: Used strictly by the Google Mobile Ads SDK to fetch banner advertisements and verify network connectivity.

4. Local File Operations & Network Access

  • Local File Operations: The App allows users to perform local file management actions (such as renaming or deleting video files). All file modifications are performed locally on your device only after explicit user confirmation.
  • Network Access: Core local video playback and media processing operate on the device, while an internet connection may be used by Google Mobile Ads to request advertisements and by user-initiated external actions such as contacting support.

5. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy periodically to reflect changes in app functionality, regulatory requirements, or Google Play Developer Policies. We encourage you to review this page periodically.
